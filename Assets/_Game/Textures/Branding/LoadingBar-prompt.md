# Loading bar concept

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Static concept only, not yet split into frame/fill/knob sprites or integrated into Splash.unity.

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a single beautiful horizontal loading bar design concept for WORD SORT, a casual mobile game themed around a turquoise refrigerator and chunky cream word magnets. Wide landscape composition, one bar only, centered, on a plain turquoise enamel backdrop. The bar is a slender cream rounded capsule frame with softly beveled glossy edges, a subtle deep purple shadow, and a recessed dark teal track. A warm golden-orange glossy fill occupies about 60 percent of the track, clean rounded leading edge. Small orange-slice magnet with a fresh green leaf attached at the leading edge of the fill, a tactile cream rim around the slice. Refined cheerful 2.5D casual mobile game art, soft highlights, simple clean silhouette, readable at small phone sizes. Overall bar approximately 7:1 width to height with orange slice a little taller than the bar, generous surrounding negative space. No text, no percentage, no logo, no extra objects, no watermark, no multiple alternatives. This is a visual concept, not a sprite sheet.
